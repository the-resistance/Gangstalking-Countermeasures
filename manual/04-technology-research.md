# Technology Research

This chapter organizes technical topics identified in [RSH-001](../research/001-voice-to-skull-v2k.md). The repository research record contains few complete external citations; consequently, topic descriptions below are research leads or hypotheses unless a verified source is specifically identified. A technology's existence does not establish its use, source, or involvement in any reported experience.

## Evidence-Status Labels

- **DOCUMENTED SCIENCE:** A finding supported by a specific, traceable scientific or authoritative source whose relevant context has been reviewed.
- **EXPERIMENTALLY DEMONSTRATED CAPABILITY:** A capability shown in a documented experiment or demonstration; this does not establish deployment in any incident.
- **PATENT CLAIM:** A description or claim in a patent. A patent is not, by itself, proof that a claimed device works as described or was built, deployed, or used.
- **RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS:** A proposed explanation or question requiring testing.
- **PERSONAL OBSERVATION:** An experience attributed to an observer; not independent technical verification.
- **UNVERIFIED CLAIM:** A statement for which adequate supporting evidence and verification are not available in the reviewed record.

These labels describe the status of a statement, not its importance. A personal observation can motivate a test without proving the mechanism being tested. Do not promote a claim to a stronger status without documenting the supporting source and review.

## Research and Attribution Limits

Keep the following questions separate:

1. Is a relevant technology described in a source?
2. Is the claimed capability experimentally demonstrated, and under what conditions?
3. Is there evidence that the technology was present or operating in a specific event?
4. Is a source or responsible party independently identified?
5. Is there evidence of intentional conduct?

Evidence for one question does not answer the others. In particular, a patent, research program, contract, or technical publication does not prove deployment in an incident. The personal audio reports in RSH-001 remain personal reports unless separately corroborated and analyzed.

## Historical and Institutional Research

Research on a historical program or institution should be grounded in its original documentary context. The existing research directory contains RSH-001; it does not contain a cited document about MKULTRA, BLUEBIRD, ARTICHOKE, a named government contract, or a specific patent. Those names are not asserted here as established links to the reported observations.

For future work, record documentary relationships rather than infer continuity from technical resemblance:

**PERSON → INSTITUTION → FUNDING → RESEARCH PROGRAM → PUBLICATION → PATENT → CONTRACT → EXPERIMENTAL APPARATUS → DOCUMENTED RESULT → FOLLOW-ON RESEARCH**

Every relationship in this chain requires documentary evidence identifying the relationship. Similar terminology or technology is not sufficient evidence of institutional or operational continuity.

# Gangstalking Countermeasures

**Khalisti | BlitzWaffe Defense** · [khalisti.ai](https://khalisti.ai)

## Purpose and research abstract

**Kevin Mahan — Las Vegas, Nevada**

This research initiative examines documented and hypothesized technological capabilities involving radio-frequency auditory phenomena, directional acoustics, electromagnetic systems, and surveillance technologies, including questions about their potential unauthorized use.

The research extends beyond technical feasibility to potential legal, ethical, psychological, social, and economic consequences of misuse. Hypothetical harms include impersonation, false attribution of communications, invasion of privacy, emotional distress, reputational injury, financial loss, mistrust, and deterioration of friendships, family relationships, and professional associations.

Establishing technological capability, actual deployment, responsible-party attribution, causation, and damages requires separate evidentiary support. Suspected misconduct is not an established finding. Alternative explanations must be considered. The initiative examines civil liability, regulatory oversight, informed consent, human-subject protections, institutional accountability, and lawful redress.

My ethical and spiritual conviction is that all human beings are God's creations and fundamentally equal. No individual, institution, government, or technology operator possesses divine authority over another person. Technological superiority does not confer moral superiority. The ability to observe, influence, or exercise power does not establish the ethical or legal right to do so.

I believe concentrated power without accountability can encourage arrogance, moral overreach, and erosion of human boundaries, including when power is transferred across generations. Knowledge should be shared to support independent thought, scientific examination, public awareness, and responsible innovation. The purpose is education, transparency, human dignity, and lawful, nonviolent accountability—not retaliation.

**Knowledge should serve humanity, never become an instrument of domination.**

## Why this matters

Unauthorized surveillance, coercion, impersonation, or experimentation can violate privacy, autonomy and human dignity. Where such conduct occurs, its consequences can extend beyond the individual to families, employment, financial security and public trust. The ethical question is not merely whether a technology works, but whether its use is justified, proportionate, authorized and accountable.

## From scientific capability to evidence

The project examines [technology research](manual/04-technology-research.md), including RF auditory phenomena, directional acoustics and electromagnetic interference. The [scientific bibliography](docs/research/PHASE-2A-SCIENTIFIC-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) and [citation audit](docs/audits/PHASE-2B-CITATION-URL-AUDIT.md) provide the source trail. A documented physical effect is distinct from evidence of its deployment, causation or responsibility in a particular event.

## From evidence to accountability

1. Define the question and distinguish observations from interpretations.
2. Evaluate scientific literature and plausible alternative explanations.
3. Preserve original records and document the limitations of any analysis.
4. Seek qualified independent review of conclusions and relevant legal requirements.
5. Pursue appropriate lawful remedies where evidence supports them, prioritizing prevention and protection rather than retaliation.

The [research and investigative field manual](MANUAL.md), [evidence preservation guidance](manual/06-evidence-preservation.md) and [Regulatory Oversight and Redress](docs/research/REGULATORY-OVERSIGHT-AND-REDRESS.md) develop these principles. The [evidence model](docs/evidence-model.md) explains how findings are classified.

## The objective

Build a coherent public research resource that advances scientific understanding, protects human dignity and supports responsible technology governance. The standard is evidence before attribution, accountability before retaliation, and knowledge in service of humanity.

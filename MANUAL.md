## Reading and Evidence Status

The chapters provide a continuous path from motivation and objectives through observations, working terminology, technical research, methods, evidence handling, and references. Supporting scenario, countermeasure, and research documents remain independently accessible and authoritative for their own records.

Descriptions of personal experience are reports, not independent technical findings. A plausible mechanism, patent, publication, or historical program does not establish a cause, source, deployment, or responsible party in any particular event. See the [terminology chapter](manual/03-terminology.md) and [technology research chapter](manual/04-technology-research.md) for the status distinctions used here.
